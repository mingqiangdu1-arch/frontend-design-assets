# DT-006 · Commerce Atelier

**A sixth, independently scoped, product-led visual system.** Designed for consumer product discovery and retail decisions—not internal SaaS management, long-form editorial reading or loud promotional landing pages.

## Visual principles

- Warm stone canvas `#F3F2ED`, white product surfaces, deep moss CTA `#254C3A`, spare modern sans typography.
- Photographic product slots can be adopted in future real integrations, with image optimization/rights checks; the demo uses original CSS product silhouettes only.
- Product discovery: filterable grids, exposed prices, visible variant choices, stock labels and a non-production bag.
- Compared with **Editorial Warm**, this style has no serif reading default, and prioritizes product scanning rather than paragraphs.
- Compared with **Refined Light SaaS**, it has no internal operations sidebar or dense record table.
