# Community conduct / 社区行为规范

Help maintain a welcoming, technically useful community. Discuss designs and evidence rather than attacking people. Respect differences in language, experience, ability and aesthetic preferences. Give constructive, specific feedback; credit original authors; never disclose another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted personal attacks, deliberate misattribution and posting confidential material are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation under GitHub's available moderation controls. Decisions should be proportionate and based on behavior. Use GitHub's reporting controls for abuse; use an established private maintainer contact if available for sensitive matters. Do not post personal details or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Contributions in Chinese and English are welcome.
